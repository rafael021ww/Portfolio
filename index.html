I'm R — an artist, student and gamer who enjoys turning ideas, curiosity and free time into something creative.

Outside of art, gaming is a big part of what I enjoy. I like getting immersed in different worlds, improving my skills, and enjoying the competitive and creative side of games.

I'm also interested in computers, technology and design. I like the process of learning something from scratch, testing it, improving it and eventually making it feel polished.
